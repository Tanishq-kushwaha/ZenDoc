## Why ZenDoc?

- **🔒 Private** — Files never leave your browser. No uploads, no servers, no accounts.
- **⚡ Fast** — All processing happens on your device.
- **📴 Offline** — Works without internet after first load.
- **💸 Free** — 27 tools, no ads, no signup, no limits.
- **🔐 Secure** — Real AES-256 encryption for PDF passwords.
